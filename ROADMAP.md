# Growing Kaal-Darshan

## Encourage repeat visits

- Publish a small, reviewed collection each week with source credits and a consistent quality bar.
- Add thematic journeys such as Buddhist routes, coastal architecture and lost capitals.
- Offer Hindi and regional-language narration with reviewed translations.
- Add classroom worksheets, guided questions and a personal exploration passport.
- Invite contributions with a review queue, rights declaration and visible edit history.
- Add low-bandwidth photo mode, keyboard-friendly model annotations and transcripts for films.

## Expand worldwide

- Keep country, coordinates, language, era and source provenance as structured fields.
- Add one region with local museum or university partners before expanding broadly.
- Commission or license measured scans. Separate present-day scans from historical hypotheses.
- Add public curated collections separately from private user contributions.
- Use durable place URLs, multilingual search, accessible captions and a media rights register.
- Track retention and successful place exploration only with a clear privacy policy and consent where required.

## Current scope

The app remains free and contains no checkout, subscription, payment webhook or paid access gate.
Focus first on trustworthy content, working account storage and useful repeat visits.
