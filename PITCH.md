# Kaal-Darshan: four-minute demo

## 0:00-0:40 - Problem

When we visit a damaged monument, we see what remains. It is harder to understand
its original spatial arrangement, how it changed, and which historical claims we can trust.
Photographs, heritage records and 3D experiences often require separate searches.

## 0:40-1:10 - Approach

Kaal-Darshan connects a place, its historical timeline and a counterfactual architectural
view. Its distinguishing principle is visible uncertainty: a plausible roof is not treated
as a verified roof. Existing archives and digitization projects are valuable source material;
we build an accessible interpretive journey around those records.

## 1:10-2:50 - Live demo

1. Search for Hampi and open Vittala Temple.
2. Orbit the surviving-form schematic and start the camera tour.
3. Switch to What if intact. Select the upper tower and read its speculative label.
4. Scrub the timeline to 1565. Explain that a city-wide conflict does not date every missing element.
5. Open History and Sources to inspect ASI and UNESCO references.
6. Open Photographs and Videos. Demonstrate supplied media or state that videos are awaiting contribution.
7. Open Manage places to show how your curated dates, notes, photos and videos enter the collection.

## 2:50-3:25 - Honest scope

This prototype has two pilot places. Its hand-built geometry is schematic and not a measured scan.
It supports user-supplied GLB models, but does not generate photogrammetry or infer historical truth.
Image search links to Google Lens. Google account sign-in activates after OAuth configuration.
The archive guide answers from local notes and declines unsupported questions.

## 3:25-4:00 - Next stage

The next investment is better evidence: licensed scans, documented reconstruction decisions and
expert review with archaeologists and universities. With heritage-agency partnerships, the same
content model could support classroom journeys, multilingual interpretation and additional sites.
The goal is responsible access to heritage, with a traceable distinction between evidence and imagination.
